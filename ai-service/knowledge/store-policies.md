# Shipping
Most orders arrive within 2-4 working days anywhere in India. You can check an estimated delivery date for your pincode on any product page.
Delivery is free on orders of Rs 499 or more; below that a flat Rs 40 delivery charge applies.

# Returns and refunds
You can return most items within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Start a return from My Orders.
Refunds reach UPI and cards within 3-5 working days after the pickup. Cash-on-delivery refunds go to your bank account.
Damaged, defective or wrong items are replaced or refunded in full - share a photo of the item and packaging with support.

# Payments
How can I pay? You can pay with UPI, a credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), or pay cash on delivery (COD) when your order arrives.
UPI and card payments are confirmed with a 6-digit one-time code (OTP) sent to your registered email. The code expires in 5 minutes. Never share it with anyone.
If money was debited but no order was placed, the bank reverses it automatically within 5-7 working days.

# Cancellation
You can cancel an order from My Orders until it ships. Refunds for cancelled orders are automatic.
Delivery address and items cannot be changed after the order is placed - cancel and order again instead.

# Order tracking
You can see the status of every order on the My Orders page.

# Warranty
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Keep your invoice for warranty claims.

# Account and sign-in
You can sign up with your email and a password (at least 8 characters with a letter and a number) or with Continue with Google.
Forgot your password? Use the login page, or sign in with Google if your account uses the same email.

# Selling on Veyra
Anyone can become a seller from the account menu (Become a Seller). Sellers manage products, stock and orders in the Seller Hub and get AI sales insights.

# Contact
Raise a ticket from the Help Center (Contact support) or chat with the shopping assistant. Email support@veyra.example.
